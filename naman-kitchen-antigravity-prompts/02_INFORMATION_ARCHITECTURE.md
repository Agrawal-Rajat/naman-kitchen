# 02 — Information Architecture & UX Flow

## Main navigation

Desktop:
- Kitchens
- Hardware
- Projects
- About
- Contact
- CTA: Book a Consultation

Mobile:
- Logo
- Menu button
- Drawer containing all links + prominent consultation CTA.

Keep navigation minimal.

## Homepage storytelling order

The homepage should tell a visual story:

**SEE → UNDERSTAND → TRUST → IMAGINE → ACT**

### 1. SEE

Hero must immediately show a premium kitchen and communicate Naman Kitchen.

Suggested hero copy:

Eyebrow:
`MODULAR KITCHENS • INDORE`

Headline:
`A smarter kitchen, made for the way you live.`

Supporting copy:
`Modern modular kitchens designed around your space, your routines and the way Indian homes actually work.`

CTA:
`Book a Free Consultation`

Secondary:
`Explore Kitchens`

### 2. UNDERSTAND

Introduce the brand philosophy.

`Designed around you. Built for everyday life.`

Explain:
- smart storage
- thoughtful layouts
- quality materials
- reliable hardware
- practical execution

### 3. TRUST

Show:
- process
- craftsmanship
- business story
- materials
- hardware
- testimonials when real testimonials are supplied

### 4. IMAGINE

Use:
- large kitchen photography
- project cards
- layout visualizations
- detail crops
- material textures

### 5. ACT

End with a highly visible consultation section.

Suggested:
`Let's design the kitchen you'll love coming home to.`

CTA:
`Start Your Kitchen`

## Mobile UX

Mobile is not a shrunken desktop.

For every section ask:
- What is the first thing the user should see?
- What can become horizontal scroll?
- What should stack?
- Which image crop is most important?
- Is the CTA reachable with one thumb?
- Can text be read without zooming?
- Is the motion still meaningful?

Use horizontal scrolling for:
- kitchen layout cards
- project cards
- hardware cards
when appropriate.

Do not use horizontal scrolling for primary navigation.

## Consultation flow

The user should be able to reach consultation in <= 2 meaningful interactions from the hero.

Form fields:
- Name
- Phone
- City
- Kitchen type/layout
- Approximate timeline
- Optional message

Keep the form short. Mark optional fields clearly.

The frontend should include validation states but no fake submission success connected to a nonexistent backend.

## Trust principles

Indian home-improvement customers need confidence.

Communicate:
- clear process
- practical design
- hardware functionality
- transparent consultation
- local relevance
- after-sales mindset

Do not use fake scarcity or fake social proof.

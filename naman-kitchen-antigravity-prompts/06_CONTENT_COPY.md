# 06 — Content & Copy Direction

## Tone

The brand should sound:
- confident
- warm
- practical
- premium
- trustworthy
- easy to understand

Avoid:
- corporate jargon
- overused luxury words
- fake urgency
- excessive English
- overly poetic copy that hides the product benefit

## Process

01 — Understand
`We learn about your space, needs, style and budget.`

02 — Design
`We turn measurements and routines into a practical kitchen plan.`

03 — Refine
`Finishes, storage, hardware and details are selected together.`

04 — Execute
`Your approved design moves from planning to production and installation.`

05 — Live
`You get a kitchen designed for everyday life.`

Do not claim exact timelines unless the company confirms them.

## About

Use the supplied story:
- Naman Kitchen began with experience in kitchen and hardware.
- Founder Akashdeep Gupta.
- Business experience includes retail projects, wholesaler/dealer network development, direct manufacturer coordination and site-level execution.
- The business expanded after 2022 into architect-driven projects, plywood/kitchen accessories, and system-kitchen/scalable business model.
- During COVID-19, the business continued through trust, relationships and discipline.

Do not invent exact dates, locations, employee counts, project counts or revenue.

## Hindi

Use Hindi selectively for emotional/local moments rather than translating every section.

Example:
`रसोई सिर्फ एक जगह नहीं — यह घर की रोज़मर्रा की धड़कन है।`

Keep primary navigation and most UI in English unless the brand specifically wants a bilingual interface.
